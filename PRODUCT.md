## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS

## Users

Family members and descendants of Dornville Jean-Louis Mandé; researchers of French Guiana and WWI colonial troops; visitors interested in Guianese and Amazonian history.

## Product Purpose

A memorial history page for Dornville Jean-Louis Mandé (1885–1950), zouave at Verdun, decorated with the Croix de Guerre avec palme, Médaille Militaire, and Légion d'Honneur, later Directeur of the prison at Cayenne. The page tells his life story, his family line, and the Oyapock/Suriname Indigenous and Maroon world he was born into, with citations to primary sources (ANOM matricule record, Livre d'Or 1924).

## Positioning

A family-authored tribute grounded in primary archival documents, combining military heroism with Guianese Indigenous and Maroon history in one narrative.

## Operating Context

Read in a browser on desktop or mobile; visitors read long-form narrative, view a family table, and consult document references. No interactivity beyond reading.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Single static HTML page with embedded CSS. Content is family and archival fact; no invented claims. The page embeds the Livre d’Or citation, the book’s title page, and the ANOM matricule, and uses four captioned evocations (river, Verdun winter, the three decorations, a Cayenne street). Those evocations are not presented as photographs of him.

## Brand Commitments

Visual direction: Guyane Amazon tropics — lush greens, river blues, earth tones, warmth, the Oyapock River, Amazon rainforest. Binding visual constraint volunteered by the user.

## Product Principles

- Facts first: every claim traceable to a named document or family record
- Respectful tone: honor the subject's rank, origin, and sacrifice without militaristic ornament
- Readability: long-form narrative must be easy to read on any screen
- Tropical Guyane identity: visual design rooted in the Amazon/Oyapock world, not generic memorial templates

## Accessibility & Inclusion

High-contrast text, readable serif body copy, semantic HTML, responsive layout for mobile and desktop.
